Institutions operating geolocation-based access controls must keep their blocked-territory configuration aligned with the comprehensive sanctions perimeter. That reconciliation is currently performed by hand, independently, at every institution, against prose legal sources of varying currency. This reference states the perimeter in the identifiers those systems use, cites a primary source for every record, and records every change.

Under-blocking is the failure everyone knows. A territory is designated, a configuration lags, prohibited transactions get through, and an enforcement action eventually follows.
Over-blocking is the failure nobody sees. When a programme is terminated, an institution that does not update its configuration continues to deny service to a jurisdiction the United States no longer comprehensively sanctions. No alert fires, because no control is designed to detect a transaction that was wrongly stopped. No metric records it, because nobody files a complaint saying they were improperly excluded. This checker reports both directions.

Limits. This reference covers comprehensive territorial programmes only. It is not a list of designated persons, it does not cover sectoral or list-based measures, and it is not legal advice. Verify against the cited primary source before relying on any record.
